Certifications & Quality

Two World-Class Standards

MBS holds both CARB Phase II and JAS certifications — giving our customers direct access to the USA, Japan, and any market that demands the highest emission and quality standards.

CARB Phase II — California Air Resources Board

Certified 2009. One of the strictest global standards for formaldehyde emissions. Required for selling composite wood products in California and regulated US markets. Cert No. TPC6/CARB-ATCM/M019-HWPW014.

🌸

JAS — Japan Agricultural Standards

Certified to Japanese Agricultural Standards — a prerequisite for entering Japan's premium timber market. Assures Japanese buyers of dimensional accuracy, bond strength, and formaldehyde compliance.

V-Legal — Indonesian Timber Legality Assurance

SVLK (Sistem Verifikasi Legalitas Kayu) verified. Confirms our timber comes from legal, sustainably managed sources — required for exporting to many key markets.

✅

Export-Grade Multi-Stage QC

Every shipment passes thickness tolerance, moisture content (8–12%), glue bond strength, and visual grading checks. Full documentation provided for customs and inspection.

Our History

The Mustikatama Group Story

MBS is part of a diversified industrial group with roots going back to 1987. A long track record of building and scaling manufacturing businesses in East Java, Indonesia.

1987
Mustikatama Group Founded
Group origins in East Java with salt iodization (CV. Mekarsari Jaya) and rice milling (CV. Sumbersari Jaya) operations.
1993
PT Mustika Bahana Jaya (MBJ)
Timber operations begin. MBJ established, specializing in rubberwood and Merkusii pine processing.
1998
Expansion to North Sumatra
PT Sumber Karindo Sakti (SKS) established in Medan — taking Finger Joint Laminated Board production nationwide.
2004
MBJ Achieves JAS Certification
Group earns its first Japan Agricultural Standards certification, opening Japan's premium timber market.
🌸 JAS Certified
2006
PT Mustika Buana Sejahtera Founded
MBS established — the Group's dedicated Albizia Falcata (Sengon) plywood and blockboard manufacturer. Powered by Group expertise and new talent.
🏭 MBS Established
2009
MBS Achieves CARB Phase II
MBS earns California Air Resources Board certification — unlocking the US market and solidifying MBS as a world-class supplier. Cert No. TPC6/CARB-ATCM/M019-HWPW014.
🏆 CARB Phase II
Today
700+ Workers, 5 Continents

Our Process

From Plantation to Your Warehouse

Every panel you receive meets the same standard as the last. Our process is built for consistency at scale.

Step 01

Plantation Sourcing

Albizia Falcata logs arrive from certified plantation forests — fast-growing, renewable, and fully traceable.

→ Traceable plantation origin
Step 02

Processing & Drying

Logs are peeled or sliced to specification, then kiln-dried to 8–12% moisture content by our Japan-trained team.

→ 8–12% MC target
Step 03

Multi-Stage QC

Thickness, glue bond strength, formaldehyde levels, and visual grading — checked at every production stage. CARB II and JAS compliant.

→ Dual-certified compliance
Step 04

Export-Ready Packing

Export-standard packing with full documentation — phytosanitary certificate, B/L, packing list, and cert copies ready for your customs.

→ Ships to 5 continents
